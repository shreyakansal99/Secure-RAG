Acme Corp Salary Bands (Confidential, HR only)
Level 1 (Junior Engineer) earns 6 to 9 lakh per year.
Level 2 (Engineer) earns 10 to 16 lakh per year.
Level 3 (Senior Engineer) earns 18 to 28 lakh per year.
Level 4 (Engineering Manager) earns 30 to 45 lakh per year.
The annual bonus ranges from 5 to 15 percent of base salary depending on performance rating.
Salary reviews take place once a year in April.
Promotions are decided by a calibration committee based on performance and scope of work.
Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible for the annual bonus.
Relocation allowance is up to 1.5 lakh for approved transfers between offices.
Salary band information must not be shared outside the HR department.
