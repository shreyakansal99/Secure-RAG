Acme Corp Salary Bands (Confidential, HR only)
Level 1 (Junior Engineer): 6 to 9 lakh per year.
Level 2 (Engineer): 10 to 16 lakh per year.
Level 3 (Senior Engineer): 18 to 28 lakh per year.
Level 4 (Engineering Manager): 30 to 45 lakh per year.
Annual bonus ranges from 5 to 15 percent of base depending on performance rating.
