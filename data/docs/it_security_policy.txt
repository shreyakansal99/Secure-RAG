Acme Corp IT Security Policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts.
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption.
Personal USB drives are not allowed on company devices.
Security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery.
A VPN is required when accessing internal systems from outside the office.
Employees must lock their screens whenever they leave their desks.
Software may only be installed from the approved company catalog.
Suspicious emails must be forwarded to the security team and never opened.
Access to internal systems is reviewed every quarter and removed when an employee leaves.
