Acme Corp Leave Policy
All full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year.
Sick leave is 12 days per year, and a doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks paid.
Paternity leave is 2 weeks paid.
Unused annual leave up to 10 days can be carried over to the next year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal.
Employees receive 10 public holidays per year, as published in the annual holiday calendar.
Bereavement leave of 5 days is granted on the death of an immediate family member.
Unpaid leave of up to 1 month may be approved by the department head in exceptional circumstances.
Leave balances can be viewed at any time in the HR portal.
